How to read this. Volumes are annual averages over 2000–2024 (the 25-year total ÷ 25), paddy-equivalent, from FAOSTAT's Detailed Trade Matrix. Shares are of imports from the 24 tracked exporters, not of a country's imports from the whole world — a supplier outside that set is invisible here.
Spare capacity is an assumption, not a measurement. It is 5% of the rice a country currently keeps for domestic use (2022–24 average), which presumes it would divert part of its own food supply to export. Only countries already exporting at least 10,000 t a year are listed. Price, freight, rice variety and import standards are not modelled.
Water stress is national. SDG 6.4.2 measures withdrawals against renewable freshwater for a whole country and cannot locate pressure inside one — India reads 66% nationally while Punjab, where much of its rice grows, is far tighter. Exact readings are shown where the team's notebooks publish them; the rest are classified above or below the 25% threshold, solved exactly from the exposure data.
This is a screening tool, not a forecast. It does not predict that any supplier will cut its exports.
